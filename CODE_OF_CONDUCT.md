# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, or abusive conduct in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@review-to-rule.dev`. Maintainers
may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose conduct harms the community. Enforcement decisions will be
made consistently and with regard for participant safety and privacy.
